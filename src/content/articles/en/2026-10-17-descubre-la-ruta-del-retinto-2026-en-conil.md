The Retinto Route is an event that no food lover wants to miss. This year, the event features the participation of 17 establishments, each presenting a unique and special take on this exquisite meat. The richness of Gaditanian cuisine truly shines on these occasions, showcasing to the world the culinary art that enriches the province.

### A Gourmet Experience in the Heart of Cádiz

Throughout the weekend, attendees will have the opportunity to embark on a culinary journey through Conil, where each dish is an expression of Cádiz's culture and love for its land. The exquisite retinta beef is the star of the show, with recipes ranging from traditional preparations to avant-garde innovations that will surprise even the most discerning palates.

The participating restaurants and bars have prepared a variety of options, showcasing the versatility of this local product. From creative tapas to more elaborate dishes, each offering provides an explosion of authentic flavors. The chefs have focused on high-quality local ingredients, reinforcing the region's commitment to sustainability and respect for the environment.

### A Feast for the Senses

More than just a gastronomic event, the Retinto Route is a celebration of the Gaditanian lifestyle: its joy, its passion for good food, and its ability to share unique moments. Conil, with its picturesque streets and welcoming atmosphere, becomes the perfect setting to enjoy this experience while appreciating the beauty of the typical Andalusian landscape.

The cultural and leisure offerings to complement the route are extensive and varied. Conil will offer activities for the whole family, making this event the perfect excuse to visit the area and enjoy the unparalleled surroundings. Moreover, the direct contact with hosts and the opportunity to learn more about the delights of retinta beef make this an educational and enriching event.

### A Journey to the Heart of Gaditanian Gastronomy

This weekend, we invite you to be part of the unique celebration that is the Retinto Route 2026. Conil de la Frontera opens its doors to showcase everything Gaditanian culture has to offer: its tasty gastronomy, its warm hospitality, and its natural charm that promises to make your visit an unforgettable experience.

Prepare your senses for a gastronomic adventure that celebrates the best of Cádiz and its rich culinary heritage. This weekend, Conil de la Frontera awaits you with open arms and ready plates!
