The Ruta del Retinto is not just a showcase of local delights; it's a cultural experience that allows visitors to immerse themselves in the authentic atmosphere of Cádiz. This year, once again, local restaurants competed to create the most exquisite tapas, primarily using retinto beef, a culinary treasure of the region known for its flavor and quality. 

In 2026, several establishments stood out as the big winners, capturing the taste buds of judges and visitors alike. Among them, the restaurant 'El Atún Dorado' took the first prize with its innovative and delicious tapa. Their creation, 'Retinto a la Brisa del Mar,' combined the tenderness of the meat with a touch of seaweed and subtle citrus undertones, a dish that perfectly embodied the fresh and flavorful spirit of Andalusian cuisine. 

Another notable establishment was 'Casa del Sur,' which presented 'Bocados de Retinto con Aroma de Patios Gaditanos,' where local spices enhanced the authentic taste of the meat. This tapa was not only a delight for the palate but also a representation of the colorful and fragrant atmosphere of the typical patios of Cádiz. 

The Ruta del Retinto is more than just a journey through flavors; it's a celebration that brings together culture, tradition, and modernity, attracting tourists from all around the world. Here in Zahara, every dish tells a story, each tapa is a window into the centuries-old traditions of the region, and every bite is filled with a love for the land and sea that can only be found in this part of the world. 

For those seeking an authentic experience during their vacation on the Costa de la Luz, the Ruta del Retinto de Zahara is a must. Beyond indulging in unique cuisine, visitors can enjoy the golden beaches, crystal-clear waters, and the warm hospitality of the people of Cádiz. There's no better place to savor the true taste of Cádiz than here, in the heart of Zahara de los Atunes.
